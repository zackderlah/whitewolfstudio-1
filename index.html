House rules

How we keep the chair on time.

Bookings & deposits

A 50% deposit is required for all bookings to secure your appointment. This deposit is applied to your service total.

Health & safety

If you are feeling unwell, please do not attend. Reschedule — it protects the team and the next client in the chair.

Punctuality

Please arrive on time. Arriving more than 15 minutes late is treated as a late cancellation, and a fee may apply.

Appointment changes

We need at least 12 hours’ notice for changes or cancellations. Changes inside 12 hours may incur a fee.

Hair preparation

Arrive with clean hair unless you have booked a wash. It is the difference between a good cut and the right one.
